## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges archival preservation with vibrant, living celebration. It stands at the intersection of historical gravitas, cultural pride, and contemporary queer joy. The aesthetic honors memory and lived experience without feeling archaic or solemn; instead, it feels warm, dignified, tactile, and visually rich.

The visual style pairs refined editorial sophistication with grounded earthiness:
- **Elevated Cultural Editorial:** Dramatic typographical rhythm evocative of luxury print journals, artist monographs, and curated galleries, driven by bold industrial impact.
- **Warm Tactility:** Clean surfaces rooted in warm sun-baked clay, linen-tinted paper, and natural sand rather than sterile corporate whites or clinical grays.
- **Discreet Cultural Geometry:** Minimalist textile-inspired accents, rhythmic hairline framing, and intentional asymmetrical compositions that reference ancestral craft in a clean, modern digital language.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by `#C2410C` (deep terracotta), evocative of red clay earth, fired ceramics, and warm architectural stone. This is complemented by vibrant secondary and accent tones that reflect celebratory queer identity and natural landscapes, operating within a rich dark mode environment:

- **Primary (`#C2410C`):** Terracotta. Used for key interactive actions, mastheads, primary highlights, and brand anchor points.
- **Secondary (`#7C3AED`):** Electric violet. Represents celebratory queer dignity, nocturnal energy, and dynamic artistic highlights. Used selectively for elevated badges, active focus states, and featured multimedia badges.
- **Tertiary (`#059669`):** Acacia emerald. Grounded, lush organic accent reserved for audio/oral archive statuses, success states, and natural rhythm separators.
- **Sunlit Accent (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber/marigold. Used sparingly for radiant hover touches, timeline markers, and joyful archival badges.
- **Neutral Primary (`#18181B`):** Obsidian ink. Ensures uncompromising structure for dark-mode editorial journalism and historical essays.
- **Neutral Backgrounds & Surfaces:** Deep charcoal and dark obsidian tones serve as the base reading plane, providing robust contrast for high-impact typography and warm surface containers.

## Typography

The typographical pairing strikes an intentional balance between high-craft archival heritage and striking contemporary presence:

- **Bebas Neue:** Chosen for its striking industrial proportions, condensed presence, and commanding visual weight. It gives essay titles, article leads, oral history quotes, and curated retrospective headers the impact of bold museum-grade publication.
- **Anybody:** A versatile, modern variable sans-serif that prevents editorial fatigue across long multi-paragraph pieces, transcriptions, timelines, and metadata tables.
- **Archivo Narrow:** Utilized for tight, condensed labels and metadata readouts with clean geometric precision.

Headings must maintain snug leading (`1.15` to `1.25`) to preserve dense editorial impact, while body type breathes freely with generous line height (`1.6` to `1.7`) over dark surfaces to maximize sustained reading comfort.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a hybrid editorial grid system:
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column asymmetric fluid grid with `2rem` gutters and `3.5rem` outer canvas margins. Long-form essays are constrained to a focused 7-column or 8-column reading track centered or offset with 4 columns reserved for primary quotes, annotations, and archival imagery.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Sidebars collapse into contextual horizontal drawers or modular inter-paragraph blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` outer canvas padding. Micro-padding ensures maximum horizontal text economy without edge tension.

Spacing rhythm follows a strict harmonic structure:
- Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for tight typographical groupings, metadata pairings, and inline chip spacing.
- Use `space-md` and `space-lg` for card internal padding and component-to-component separation.
- Use `space-xl`, `space-2xl`, and `space-3xl` exclusively for macro section divisions, photo essay spacing, and chapter demarcations.

## Elevation & Depth

This dark mode system avoids synthetic harsh drop shadows. Visual depth is rendered through material layering and rich, diffuse ambient light over dark foundations:

- **Tonal Stepping:** Depth is primarily established by shifting dark container surfaces. Layer 1 cards, reading modules, and audio player docks lift slightly onto lighter or darker container tiers.
- **Ambient Terracotta Glow:** When floating components require physical separation (e.g., sticky archival players, contextual glossaries, modal dialogues), shadows are warm and heavily diffused rather than neutral black:
  - *Standard Card:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.1)`
  - *Elevated Archive Floating Sheet:* `0 12px 32px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.2)`
- **Structural Hairstyle Borders:** Ghost borders in subtle muted tones maintain crisp architectural definition without heaviness.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture uses highly rounded, friendly, and tactile curvature across all interactive elements. 

- **Level 3 (Pill-shaped / Highly Rounded):** Foundation elements (cards, media viewports, text input fields, and modal containers) adhere to generous corner rounding (`1rem` to `2rem`), scaling up to full pill shapes (`rounded-full` or `9999px`) for buttons, metadata badges, category tags, oral recording controls, and interactive pill components. This produces a soft, modern, and highly approachable architectural tension.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Terracotta background (`#C2410C`), crisp typography, fully rounded pill format (`rounded-full`) when expressing celebratory joy. Hover transitions gently into rich burnt sienna with subtle y-axis translation (`-1px`).
- **Secondary / Archive Action:** Transparent surface with a 1.5px border in `#C2410C` or `#7C3AED`, body font in label-lg weight, transitioning to a light terracotta wash on hover.
- **Ghost / Minimal:** Plain text styling with a subtle bottom hairline that expands outward from center on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Badges:** Rendered as full pills with `0.5rem` horizontal padding, featuring high-contrast dark-mode friendly pairings (e.g., Electric Violet `#7C3AED` tint for queer history entries; Acacia `#059669` tint for recorded testimonies).
- **Filter Chips:** Subdued dark surface background, borderless in resting state, active state highlighted with solid Terracotta (`#C2410C`) and contrasting text.

### Lists & Timeline Entries
- **Archival Feed:** Separated by 1px muted hairlines. Each list item incorporates a small terracotta or amber accent indicator marking time period or geographical origin.
- **Oral History Index:** Displays speaker portrait/audio waveform preview, title in `Bebas Neue`, and accompanying metadata aligned via tabular numerals.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Archival Retrieval:** Set on dark container surfaces, bounded by a subtle 1px border. On focus, shifts to a 1.5px border in `#C2410C` with a warm ambient ring (`rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.25)`). Placeholder copy is rendered in muted ink.

### Cards & Media Portals
- **Feature Card:** Minimal dark surface container sitting atop the background canvas. Framing uses 1px soft borders. Imagery uses rich, full-bleed aspect ratios (`16:10` or `4:5`) accompanied by `Bebas Neue` headlines and high-contrast metadata.
- **Quote Card:** Distinct container featuring an exaggerated quotation mark in terracotta at 20% opacity, paired with medium italicized serif text.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Oral Narrative Player:** A persistent bottom or inline audio player docking station featuring fluid terracotta wave tracks, marigold timestamp handles, and electric violet state badges indicating transcribed dialect availability.
- **Archival Artifact Viewer:** Deep dark obsidian backdrop (`#18181B`) with warm controls, allowing zoom, translation toggles, and provenance verification.
